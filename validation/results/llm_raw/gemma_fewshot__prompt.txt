language_skills — Language Skills: field courses that develop the student teacher's own command of English: reading, writing, listening and pronunciation, oral communication, grammar and the structure of English, vocabulary, critical reading and writing, expression skills, translation between Turkish and English, English for media or academic purposes. A field course on teaching one of these skills (for example teaching language skills or teaching vocabulary) also belongs here.

linguistics — Linguistics: field courses that study language scientifically: linguistics, phonetics and phonology, morphology, syntax, semantics, pragmatics, sociolinguistics, discourse analysis, language acquisition, language and society, varieties of English. literature — Literature: field courses on literature written in English, including courses on using literature in language teaching.

pedagogy — Pedagogy: teaching-profession courses (Meslek Bilgisi) such as introduction to education, educational psychology, sociology, philosophy and history of education, instructional principles and methods, instructional technologies, classroom management, research methods in education, measurement and evaluation, ethics in education, the Turkish education system and school management, guidance, special education and inclusion, school experience and teaching practice, and their electives (for example open and distance learning, museum education, inclusive education, character and values education, adult education, learning disabilities, child psychology, critical and analytical thinking, drama in education); and ELT methodology courses not tied to one content domain (approaches and methods in English language teaching, teaching English to young learners, ELT curricula, materials design and evaluation, textbook evaluation, testing and assessment in ELT, course content development, drama or technology in ELT).

general_ed — General Education: general-culture courses (Genel Kültür) such as Atatürk's principles and the history of the Turkish revolution, Turkish language, foreign languages other than English (for example German as a second foreign language), information technologies or computer literacy, community service practice, and general-culture electives (for example arts and aesthetics, health and nutrition, economics and entrepreneurship, media literacy, human rights, human relations and communication, career planning, history and philosophy of science, research ethics, culture and language, Turkish music, crafts, folk dance or sign language, professional English offered as a general-culture elective), and university-wide common electives.
